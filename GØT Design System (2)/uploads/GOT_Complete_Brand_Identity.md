## 2. Brand Strategy

### Brand essence

**FORGED TO BE DIFFERENT** — individuality, edge, self-expression, confidence and an intentionally selective identity

### Brand promise — recommended interpretation

GØT offers streetwear for people who choose their own identity instead of following the crowd. The product is more than a hoodie: it is a statement of attitude and belonging

### Brand personality — recommended

- Bold, self-assured, independent
- Dark, minimal, sharp, controlled
- Rebellious without visual clutter
- Premium in presentation, grounded in street culture
- Mysterious and anticipation-driven during pre-launch

### Target audience — working hypothesis

Fashion-conscious young adults interested in dark minimalist streetwear, distinctive symbols, statement hoodies, limited drops and social-first fashion. Exact ages, price sensitivity and geographic distribution need customer research

### Differentiators to explore — not verified claims

- Iconic sword-and-monogram identity
- Consistent black-and-silver unboxing system
- Drop-led releases and scarcity storytelling
- Physical branded collectibles such as keychains

Do not claim limited production, superior fabric quality, local manufacturing, exclusivity or sustainability until documented

- Uppercase for headlines and labels; generous letter spacing
- Keep headline length short, typically 2–6 words
- English-first observed in source material; Arabic localization can be designed separately

## 4. Visual Identity

### 4.1 Logo system — observed

The core mark combines a serif **G** on the left, a stylized **Ø/O** on the right, and a vertically centered medieval-style sword. A sharp diagonal slash intersects the right-hand letter. The sword extends above and below the letterforms and is the defining visual anchor

**Observed variations:**

1. White/silver distressed monogram on black
2. Metallic embossed/engraved keychain version
3. Small white monogram on black packaging

**Recommended logo usage:**

- Primary: silver/white sword monogram on near-black
- Secondary: flat white monogram on solid black
- Merchandise: etched or cast silver/steel finish
- Icon: sword or complete compact monogram only after approval
- Keep proportions, sword alignment, and slash angle consistent
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, add gradients, or overlay busy photos without contrast

**Clear space — proposed:** maintain a margin of at least 15% of the full logo width around the artwork  
**Minimum size — proposed:** 32 px digital for full mark only if visually legible; use larger for detailed sword features  
**Source artwork needed:** vector SVG/AI/PDF and a transparent PNG from the original designer; the supplied images are raster mockups and are not reliable production-ready vector masters

### 4.2 Color palette

### 4.3 Typography — recommended

The supplied mockups use spaced uppercase geometric/grotesque-looking sans-serif lettering for headlines and supporting copy; the exact typeface cannot be identified reliably from the images

- **Headlines:** Neue Haas Grotesk / Helvetica Now / Inter Tight (depending on license)
- **Body and UI:** Inter / Manrope
- **Utility text:** IBM Plex Mono sparingly for drop numbers, dates and countdowns
- **Logo:** custom artwork; never recreate with a stock font
- **Tracking:** increased on short uppercase headlines, normal on body text
- **Hierarchy:** large restrained headlines, short descriptions, clear CTAs

### 4.4 Art direction

- Low-key, directional studio lighting with hard-edged shadows
- Matte black paper and black reflective plastic
- Silver metal and weathered-steel textures
- Clean monochrome backgrounds, negative space and central framing
- Macro detail shots of sword, chain, packaging folds and print texture
- Editorial, cinematic, high-contrast product photography
- Avoid bright multicolor gradients, playful cartoon iconography, neon accents and crowded compositions

## 7. Digital Brand Experience

### Website goals — proposed

1. Communicate brand identity in under five seconds
2. Build anticipation for Drop 01
3. Collect opt-in launch notifications
4. Direct visitors to verified social accounts
5. Provide contact and basic brand information
6. Prepare for a future commerce launch without falsely implying items are available

### Coming Soon page — recommended structure

**Hero**

- Dark full-screen visual with large GØT logo
- Eyebrow: EST. 2026 / ALEXANDRIA, EGYPT
- Heading: FORGED TO BE DIFFERENT
- Supporting line: DROP 01 — COMING SOON
- CTA: GET EARLY ACCESS / NOTIFY ME

**Below the fold**

- Brand statement: MORE THAN JUST A HOODIE
- Editorial preview of approved product/packaging photography
- Optional teaser for first drop — only if assets are approved
- Email signup with consent and confirmation state
- Instagram / TikTok / verified Facebook links
- Contact email and footer

**Do not add a countdown with an invented launch date**. Only enable a countdown when the launch time is confirmed

### UX and UI direction

- Mobile-first, responsive, accessible
- Minimal black navigation, high-contrast text, precise spacing
- Large hero typography and restrained motion
- Subtle metallic sheen or reveal on the sword mark only when performance permits
- Reduced-motion support
- Clear keyboard focus and accessible signup validation
- No autoplay audio or disruptive intro screen

### Recommended technical checklist

- Confirm IDN domain handling for `gøteg.com` and its ASCII/Punycode representation
- Verify domain ownership, DNS, SSL, www redirects and canonical URL
- Configure a domain email when ready; current Gmail address may remain for support
- Add metadata, Open Graph image, favicon, robots and sitemap as appropriate
- Use properly compressed WebP/AVIF images and responsive sizes
- Add spam protection and privacy-compliant signup storage
- Explicit opt-in and unsubscribe process for marketing emails
- Verify all QR destinations, social URLs and contact links
- Connect analytics only with appropriate consent and privacy disclosures
- Do not claim product stock, delivery dates or launch dates without confirmation

## 11. Brand Governance

### Recommended approval workflow

1. Confirm official brand name and trademark status
2. Approve final master logo and vector variants
3. Approve final print and digital color specifications
4. Confirm typography and licensing
5. Confirm official handles, domain and contact channels
6. Approve tone-of-voice rules and all taglines
7. Proof packaging and scan every QR
8. Approve website content, consent and launch date

### Never assume

- That ® is legally registered
- That `gøteg.com` is active or owned by the brand
- That a supplied phone is active on WhatsApp
- That mockup QR codes resolve correctly
- That packaging mockups represent manufactured products
- That follower counts remain current
- That exact font families or HEX codes can be recovered from photos alone
