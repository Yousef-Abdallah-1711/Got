# Quickstart: Wishlist and Guest-to-Account Merge

1. As a guest, save a product from its card → confirm the header count updates and the same product's heart is filled on its product page too.
2. Open the dedicated wishlist page → confirm the saved product appears with current price/availability.
3. Set that product's stock to 0 → reload the wishlist page → confirm it's marked unavailable and "move to cart" is blocked.
4. Save a single-variation product, click "move to cart" → confirm it's added directly; save a multi-variation one, click "move to cart" → confirm it routes to the product page instead.
5. As the same guest, save a second product in a new tab → confirm the first tab's count updates too (cross-tab sync).
6. Register a new account → confirm both guest-saved products now appear in the account's wishlist with no duplicates.
7. Log in to an existing account that already has one overlapping saved product from a different guest session → confirm the merged list has no duplicate.
8. Remove a product from the store entirely while it's wishlisted → confirm it shows as "no longer available" with only a remove option.
9. Clear the wishlist → confirm an explicit confirmation step is required before items are removed.

**Done when**: all 9 steps pass.
