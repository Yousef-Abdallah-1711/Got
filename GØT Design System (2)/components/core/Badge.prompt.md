```jsx

<Badge tone="soldout">Sold out</Badge>
<Badge tone="drop">Drop 01</Badge>
<Badge tone="bogo">Buy 1 Get 1</Badge>
<Badge tone="shipping" icon="truck">Free shipping</Badge>
<Badge tone="low">Low stock</Badge>
```
