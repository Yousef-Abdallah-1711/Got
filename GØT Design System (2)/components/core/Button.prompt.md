Uppercase rectangular CTA — primary is solid inverse (off-white on dark, black on light) and inverts to outline on hover.
```jsx
<Button size="lg" fullWidth>Add to cart</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" arrow>Shop Drop 01</Button>
<Button variant="link">Size guide</Button>
<Button loading>Placing order</Button>
```
- One primary per view. Labels are functional (ADD TO CART, CHECKOUT, GET EARLY ACCESS) — never a tagline.
- Radius 2px. Sizes 44/48/56 px — all meet 44px hit target.
